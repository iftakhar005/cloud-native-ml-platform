Thikana prototype

Splits a Bangladeshi address, typed in Bangla or English, into its parts.

Please read before typing

Do not type a real person's address. Use the examples or made-up ones. This app does not store what you type and logs only counts, never the text. It runs on a free public host, and that host may keep its own logs of requests, which this app cannot see or control. There are no cookies, no analytics and nothing is loaded from other sites.
